## YouTube / Devpost description (copy and paste)
ScamShield BD is a free AI web app that helps everyday people in Bangladesh spot scam messages before they lose money, and recover if they already have.

- Check a message: paste an SMS, Messenger text or bKash notification, or upload a screenshot, and get a verdict, a risk score, the red flags and what to do next.
- Explain in Bangla: one tap turns the result into simple Bangla to share with family.
- Report scam numbers: flag a sender so others can see how many people reported it.
- Practice game: AI-generated messages teach you to tell scams from genuine ones.
- Get Help: describe what happened and get a personal recovery plan, then ask follow-up questions grounded in your own incident.
- Works in English and Bangla, on mobile and desktop, with dark mode, and installs like an app. Check history stays on your device.
- Live AI agent chat: talk through a suspicious message or a scam in real time, in English or Bangla.

Live app: https://scamshield.najmunnaharhira.me
